# Habits

One heading per habit. **Target** is days per week — be honest, not
aspirational; a target you hit builds the habit, a target you miss builds
the guilt. The **Log** line is just dates. The page's "Done today" button
writes them, and so does the evening message on your phone, which carries a
button for each habit you haven't ticked yet. Or add them by hand.

A habit with **Steps** is a routine: several small things that happen as one
moment, with one tick between them. **Floor** is the version that survives a
bad day — a hotel, a 1am arrival, a morning on campus. On a day that goes
sideways the floor counts as the tick, which is what stops a run of misses
from ending the whole thing. **When** is the hour it belongs to; for a
routine, the phone asks you there. **Cue** is the moment it follows ("after
brushing my teeth at night"): an hour changes from house to house, and a cue
comes with you.

A habit you aim for every day comes with two **spare days** a week, the dots
beside its count. A missed day spends one, and a week with both spent still
counts as kept. Add a **Spares** line to change how many (0 turns them off).
For the first three days after you change houses, every habit with a floor
shows the floor: habits hang on the cues of a place, and the new place
doesn't have them yet.
