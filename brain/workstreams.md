# Workstreams

Everything you have on, one heading each. This file is the whole system — the
page, the map and the briefing are all just views of it.

Each workstream is a `## ` heading plus a few `- **Field:** value` lines:

- **Status:** Moving | Stalled | Blocked | Waiting | Not started | Done | Dropped | Parked
- **Ball:** Me | Them — who, exactly | Nobody
- **Area:** Dad | School | Business | Personal (or anything else)
- **Due:** YYYY-MM-DD — only if there is a real date
- **Touched:** YYYY-MM-DD — the last day YOU did anything on it
- **Since:** YYYY-MM-DD — when the ball last changed hands (this powers the chase reminders)
- **Next:** the single next physical action
- **Why:** one line on why this matters, for the days you forget

Tasks are `- [ ]` lines. Three suffixes change how they rank:

- `(due YYYY-MM-DD)` — when the thing happens or is owed.
- `(by YYYY-MM-DD)` — the last day *acting* still works, when that is earlier.
  Left off, the verb decides: booking travel assumes a fortnight, an
  application three weeks, prep for a meeting two days.
- `(urgent)` — your own word for it, worth about "in the next two days".

A date written only in the words ("the trip 20-25 Aug") is invisible to the
ranking; Today lists those under *What the ranking can't see*.

Daily habits (exercise, food, steps) live in `habits.md`, not here.
