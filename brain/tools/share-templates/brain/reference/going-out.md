# Going out — your taste, and how to scout for you

Load this when scouting events (`/scout`), suggesting an outing, judging
whether a lineup or a show is worth an evening, or drafting an invite
around one.

**This file starts empty on purpose.** It is the difference between a
listings dump and a shortlist worth reading, and only you can fill it.
Tell Claude what you actually like and it writes the profile here — the
more specific and the more negative, the better it works. Things worth
saying out loud:

- **Music.** Genres, yes, but better: the tempo and the feeling. "Nothing
  harder than house", "melody and a voice, never pure percussion", "bands
  that play dance music". Name ten artists you would cross town for, and
  three you are tired of being recommended.
- **Rooms.** Big shows or small ones, seated or standing, open-air or
  basement, four in the morning or home by one.
- **Everything that isn't music.** Exhibitions, talks, fairs, sport,
  markets, film. What you would go to alone, and what needs a group.
- **The hard noes.** These do more work than the yeses.
- **Money.** What a normal ticket costs you, and what makes one worth
  three times that.

If you have a music-streaming export, hand it over — a library read as a
whole says more than a list of favourites, and the profile it produces is
the thing that makes a scout feel like it knows you.

## Where you are

Scouting is useless for the wrong city. Keep the places you spend time in
about-me.md; the scout reads it and works from wherever you are that week.
Note the practical bits too — which side of town is easy to get home from,
which venues need booking weeks ahead.

## Scouting rules (`/scout`)

- **Weekly, not daily.** The night shift can carry it; it self-gates on
  the `updated:` date in `brain/events.md` and skips if that is under six
  days old.
- Window: the coming ~6 weeks, from wherever you are. Public listings and
  official venue or ticket pages only — no accounts, no logins, no
  scraping.
- Rewrite `brain/events.md` each time: drop past items, cap ~30, curate
  hard (at most one club night per weekend — a dump is not a shortlist).
  Mark anything unverified `(unconfirmed)`.
- **Every item needs a booking URL**, last on the line. The page turns it
  into the Book button, and a listing you then have to go and search for
  is half a listing.
- **Surface only.** Never book, buy, subscribe, or set an alert. If a find
  fits a season item naming people, the normal drafting rules apply —
  draft it, never send it.
- Slotting a day in season.md is the owner's call. Name the fit; write
  nothing there.
