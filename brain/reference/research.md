# The research behind the brain's rules

The research reports you run on how you organise yourself, one section each:
what held up, how strong the evidence is, and what the brain took from it.
Load this when a habit, planning or coaching rule is questioned or about to
change.

Research tools grade their own evidence generously and often cite app
companies' blogs. File the corrected grade.
