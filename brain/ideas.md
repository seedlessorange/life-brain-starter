# The idea shelf

New ventures land here, not in workstreams.md. The shelf gives each
idea a home without letting it tax the projects already running.
Nothing here decays, nags, or goes overdue.

The rules:

- A new idea gets a dated line and a paragraph, written the day it
  strikes.
- It becomes a workstream only if you still want it **two weeks
  later** and can say what it displaces.
- Ideas can die here with dignity: mark them `dead` with one line on
  why. A dead idea on the shelf is a decision; a vanished one is a
  loose end.
