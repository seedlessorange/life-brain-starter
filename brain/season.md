# Season — the things you want this stretch of life to hold

One `## <season name>` heading — the current season of your life, with an
end date. Under it, the bucket: the fun you want to have had before it ends.
Nothing here nags or goes overdue; the only pressure is the number of
weekends left.

- **From:** and **Until:** bound the season; **Why:** is one line on what
  makes it special.
- An item can carry `(with: names)`, a loose intention like `(when: October)`,
  and the firm slot the page writes when you drag it onto a day —
  `(planned: 2026-10-17)` or `(planned: 2026-10-17..2026-10-18)`. Tick it
  when it happened.
