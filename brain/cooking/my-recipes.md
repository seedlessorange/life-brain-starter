# My recipes

Your own recipes: things you found, were told or worked out. On the
Cook page they sit beside any extracted cookbooks, and you can plan
meals and shopping lists from them.

Copy this shape. Only the `## title` and the **Ingredients** list
are needed; `Total:` makes it count as quick, `Serves` feeds the
shopping quantities, and `<sub>…</sub>` groups it.

## Egg drop soup
<sub>Fast dinners</sub>

Ninety seconds of cooking, from things that keep in the cupboard.

Serves 1 · Total: 5 minutes

**Ingredients**

- 500 ml chicken stock
- 2 eggs
- 1 spring onion, sliced

**Method**

**1.** Bring the stock to a rolling boil in a saucepan.

**2.** Beat the eggs and pour them in a thin stream while stirring
one way. They cook the moment they hit.

**3.** Spring onion, and plenty of pepper.
