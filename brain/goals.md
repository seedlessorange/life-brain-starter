# Goals — the finish lines you set for yourself

One `## <project name>` per project (the names of the project pages), milestones
as checkboxes. `(due ...)` takes a date, a range, or words — "mid-September",
"end of October". Add them from a project page's Goals box or by hand here.
A goal past its date makes its whole project shout, on purpose.
