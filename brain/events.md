# Out there — what's on, matched to your taste

The Season tab renders this under the bucket list. `/scout` rewrites
it about once a week from the profile in
reference/going-out.md — fill that in first, or the shortlist is
just listings.

One line per thing, with its booking page last. The page turns the
link into a Book button, and a single date into a one-click "add to
my season":

- 2026-10-09 — Who, where (why it suits you) — price — https://...
- 2026-10-08..2026-10-11 — A run of dates, venue — https://...

Sections are yours to name; Club nights / Concerts / Exhibitions /
Tech & learning / Fairs & one-offs / Watching for is a good start.
Past items drop off the page on their own.
